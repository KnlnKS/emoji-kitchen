# Emoji Kitchen privacy policy

Effective date: September 21, 2026

Emoji Kitchen is an offline Android app and optional sticker keyboard developed by Kunalan Kevin Subagaran (KKS - Games).

## Data collection

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, or internet permission. Emoji artwork is included with the installation.

## Data on your device

Favorites and recently used combinations are saved in the app's private storage and shared between the app and its keyboard. Temporary sticker images are cached locally for copying and sending. You can remove this data through Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app.

Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings and backup provider's policies. The developer does not receive these backups.

## Keyboard and sharing

The optional keyboard sends only the sticker you select to the app you are using, or copies that sticker to the system clipboard when image insertion is unavailable. It does not read or record text typed into other apps. Copied or sent stickers are handled by Android and the receiving app under their own privacy practices.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from children or other users.
