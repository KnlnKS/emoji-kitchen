# Emoji Kitchen privacy policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Emoji Kitchen is an Android app and optional sticker keyboard with offline browsing, developed by Kunalan Kevin Subagaran (KKS - Games).

## Data collection

The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not send personal information to the developer. Emoji artwork is included with the installation for offline use.

## Artwork downloads

When you copy or send a sticker while connected to the internet, the app downloads its original-resolution artwork from this project's public GitHub repository, unless it is already cached. GitHub receives the requested artwork URL and standard connection information such as your IP address. The request identifies the selected sticker; it does not include typed text, your favorites, your history, or account credentials. Browsing and searching the bundled collection do not make network requests.

Downloaded artwork is cached on your device. If a download fails or you are offline, the app uses a cached original or the bundled image.

## Data on your device

Favorites and recently used combinations are saved in the app's private storage and shared between the app and its keyboard. Temporary sticker images are cached locally for copying and sending. You can remove this data through Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app.

Android may back up or transfer app data according to your device settings and backup provider's policies. The developer does not receive these backups.

## Keyboard and sharing

The optional keyboard sends only the sticker you select to the app you are using, or copies that sticker to the system clipboard when image insertion is unavailable. It does not read or record text typed into other apps. Copied or sent stickers are handled by Android and the receiving app under their own privacy practices.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from children or other users.
