# English (United States)

## App name

Emoji Kitchen

## Short description

Mix emoji into stickers, explore combinations, and share with an offline keyboard.

## Full description

Pick two emoji and discover their sticker combination. Emoji Kitchen brings more than 151,000 combinations and 621 base emoji to your phone, available offline after installation.

Combine your favorites, try a random pair, or explore all the combinations for one emoji. Save stickers you love and return to your recently used combinations.

Enable the optional Emoji Kitchen keyboard to send stickers in apps that support images. In other apps, tap a sticker to copy it to your clipboard. Favorites and recents stay in sync between the app and keyboard.

Features:
- Offline sticker collection included with the app
- Combine and explore modes
- Random combinations
- Shared favorites and recents
- Optional sticker keyboard
- System light and dark themes
- No ads, accounts, or analytics

The complete sticker collection makes the download larger than a typical keyboard. Android 9 or newer is required.

This is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Google. Emoji Kitchen combination artwork belongs to Google; base emoji are from Noto Emoji. The app's source code is available on GitHub.
